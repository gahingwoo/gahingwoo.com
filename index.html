    About

    Linux runs most of the world's servers and every Android phone. Every change to it is reviewed in public by a maintainer who can refuse it. Five of mine were accepted into Linux 7.3, and the review threads are linked below.

    Year 13 student at Mount Albert Grammar School in Auckland, working on firmware and embedded Linux, mostly on Rockchip silicon that nobody had brought up yet. Beyond the kernel, the first OP-TEE port for the RK3576 and a Trusted Firmware-A secure-boot fix are merged too.

    Every claim on this site links to the public record behind it. Where an earlier claim of mine turned out to be wrong, the record says so.

    What is next

    RK3576 NPU
    Into the accel treeThe 14-patch series is at v11 with the driver's author reviewing. The goal is for it to be applied, so the RK3576 NPU works on a stock kernel.
    Mesa
    Depthwise, pointwise and image-input convolutions!43804 covers one regular convolution on purpose. The layer types MobileNet is actually built from follow in later series.
    charsiu
    An open runtime that matches or beats the vendor'sNine models already run end to end on the mainline driver with output identical to the CPU reference. The goal is performance at or above the closed vendor stack, with no vendor code in the path.
    Video encoder
    Rebase the VEPU510 driver on the DRM/Vulkan-Video splitThe stateful V4L2 interface was abandoned on the maintainers' advice; the driver returns once the RK3588 split it will sit on has landed.
    UEFI
    A complete UEFI for the RK3576edk2-rk3576 boots a mainline Fedora 44 desktop today. The goal is a full, polished firmware: every board it builds for verified, and the platform support upstreamable.
