About

Year 13 student at Mount Albert Grammar School in Auckland, working on firmware and embedded Linux, mostly on Rockchip silicon that nobody had brought up yet. Five of my commits are in mainline Linux 7.3, applied by the IOMMU, Rockchip and networking maintainers. The first OP-TEE port for the RK3576 and a Trusted Firmware-A secure-boot fix are merged too.

Every claim on this site links to the public record behind it. Where an earlier claim of mine turned out to be wrong, the record says so.
